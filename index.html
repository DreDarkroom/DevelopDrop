How to play

Play
a w s e d f t g y h u j k o l p are a keyboard (a row of piano). Drag on the picture to squeegee the fog: it smears, squeaks and opens the filter. 1 2 3 change the light (and the mode).
Build and drop
Hold space (or the right mouse button, or the build button) and the music thins out and climbs. Let go and the drop lands on the next beat. Shift + space builds the other way: the mix sinks under water. Quantise decides when the drop lands (now, the next beat, the next bar). Build slowly and quietly: there is no rush.
Shape a loop
Click the grid to write the bass line; right-click a step to clear it. Click dots on the rings to change the three drummers; − + change hits, ‹ › change loop length. n clears the bass line, Shift + n the drums. z x v b mute kick, snare, hats and bass (right-click a ring to mute it). Drift lets it wander around your loop: 0 keeps it exactly as written.
Styles
The style menu (or ; and ') swaps in a whole starting point: tempo, drum kit, patterns, sound and picture. Changes land on the next bar. Some are written in the spirit of artists that inspired this; none copy a track. Slow Build starts with just a kick and hats.
Tempo
The slider moves in tenths. dnb (or \) switches it to the drum and bass range up to 180. [ ] nudge by 1, { } by 0.1.
Clips, loops and recordings
● drums / ● bass (or r) record 1 to 8 bars from the next bar and loop them. Drag a saved clip, a loop .json or a performance .sqz onto the page and it snaps in on the next bar (recordings take over the page until you press back to live).
Journey
journey (or i) slowly raises the tempo while the picture changes and thickens, over as many bars as you choose.
Keep a loop
save loop stores it in this browser. ↺ saved loop puts the bass line back. files exports or imports loops as small .json files.
Show
` (backtick) hides the panel. c goes fullscreen with nothing but the picture. q changes picture quality. Right-clicks are yours: no browser menu gets in the way of a screen recording. Add ?clean=1 to the address for OBS.
Record
Pick a format, press ● rec. Lean WebM is picture and sound in a small file. Visuals only has no sound. Screen records the whole page with its controls. Video + performance saves a video and a replay file together. WAV is lossless, MP3 is 320 kbps, Performance is a tiny file that replays on the page. snap saves a still.
MIDI
midi (or m) connects a controller: learn any button or knob, or run the quick map. Needs Chrome or Edge.
Hidden
Some things are not on any button. Try 0.
