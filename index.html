How to play

Play
a w s e d f t g y h u j k o l p are a keyboard (a row of piano). Hold space to build tension, let go for the drop. Drag on the picture to squeegee the fog: it smears, squeaks and opens the filter. 1 2 3 change the light (and the mode).
Shape a loop
Click the grid to write the bass line. Click dots on the rings to change the three drummers; − + change hits, ‹ › change loop length. Drift lets it wander around your loop: 0 keeps it exactly as written.
Keep a loop
save loop stores it in this browser. ↺ saved loop puts the bass line back to what you saved. files exports or imports loops as small .json files (whole, or just bass, drums or sound).
Show
` (backtick) hides the panel, keeping the title. c goes fullscreen with nothing but the picture. q changes picture quality. Add ?clean=1 to the address for OBS.
Record
Pick a format, press ● rec. WAV is lossless, MP3 is 320 kbps, Performance is a tiny file you can replay in the player, Video keeps picture and sound. snap saves a still.
